Definitions & main properties

Each numbered entry is based on the matching statement in the paper. Notes summarize the definition or the role of the result; see the original paper for all hypotheses and proofs.

Definition

6.1 · Polyak-Lojasiewicz Condition

The PL inequality bounds the function-value gap by the squared gradient norm, without assuming convexity.

(1/2)‖∇f(x)‖² ≥ μ[f(x)−f*].

Source: Khademi (2026), Definition 6.1, p. 4239 ↗

Property

6.2 · Strong Convexity Implies PL

Strong convexity implies the Polyak–Łojasiewicz inequality.

Source: Khademi (2026), Property 6.2, p. 4239 ↗

Property

6.3 · L-smooth Strong Star Quasi-Convexity Implies PL

Under L-smoothness, strong star quasi-convexity provides a PL inequality.

Source: Khademi (2026), Property 6.3, p. 4239 ↗

Property

6.4 · PL Implies Invexity

PL-based gradient domination excludes nonoptimal critical points and implies invexity.

Source: Khademi (2026), Property 6.4, p. 4239 ↗

Property

6.5 · Strong Quasar-Convexity Implies PL

Strong (μ,γ)-quasar-convexity yields a PL bound with parameter γ²μ.

Source: Khademi (2026), Property 6.5, p. 4240 ↗

Definition

6.6 · Łojasiewicz Inequality with Exponent θ

Łojasiewicz gradient domination allows a general exponent θ in the objective gap.

(1/2)‖∇f(x)‖² ≥ μ[f(x)−f*]^(2θ), θ∈[0,1).

Source: Khademi (2026), Definition 6.6, p. 4240 ↗

Property

6.7 · Łojasiewicz with θ = 1/2 Implies PL

The exponent θ=1/2 in the Łojasiewicz inequality specializes to PL.

Source: Khademi (2026), Property 6.7, p. 4240 ↗

Property

6.9 · Local Strong Convexity under PL Condition

Relates the PL condition, bounded minimizer set, and local curvature under extra smoothness hypotheses.

Source: Khademi (2026), Property 6.9, p. 4240 ↗

Definition

6.10 · Error Bound Condition

An error bound links the magnitude of the gradient to distance from the minimizer set.

‖∇f(x)‖ ≥ μ‖x−xπ‖.

Source: Khademi (2026), Definition 6.10, p. 4241 ↗

Definition

6.11 · Quadratic Growth

Quadratic growth bounds the objective gap below by the squared distance to the minimizer set.

f(x)−f* ≥ (μ/2)‖x−xπ‖².

Source: Khademi (2026), Definition 6.11, p. 4241 ↗

Definition

6.12 · Hölder Error Bound

A Hölder error bound generalizes quadratic growth with exponent q≥1.

f(x)−f* ≥ (μ/q)‖x−xπ‖^q.

Source: Khademi (2026), Definition 6.12, p. 4241 ↗

Property

6.13 · Quadratic Growth of Strongly Quasar-Convex Functions

Strong quasar-convexity supplies a quadratic growth bound.

Source: Khademi (2026), Property 6.13, p. 4241 ↗

Property

6.14 · QG of Strongly Star Quasi-Convex Functions

Strong star quasi-convexity supplies a quadratic growth bound.

Source: Khademi (2026), Property 6.14, p. 4242 ↗

Property

6.15 · QG and Quasar-Convexity Imply Strong Quasar-Convexity

Combining quasar-convexity and quadratic growth yields a strong quasar-type condition under the stated parameters.

Source: Khademi (2026), Property 6.15, p. 4242 ↗

Definition

6.16 · Restricted Secant Inequality

The restricted secant inequality bounds gradient alignment with the direction to the closest minimizer.

⟨∇f(x),x−xπ⟩ ≥ μ‖x−xπ‖².

Source: Khademi (2026), Definition 6.16, p. 4242 ↗

Property

6.17 · First-Order Characterization of Strong Star Quasi-Convexity

A radial gradient inequality characterizes strong star quasi-convexity.

Source: Khademi (2026), Property 6.17, p. 4242 ↗

Property

6.18 · Strong Quasar-Convexity Implies RSI

A parameterized strong quasar-convexity assumption yields restricted secant inequality.

Source: Khademi (2026), Property 6.18, p. 4243 ↗

Definition

6.19 · Essential Strong Convexity

Essential strong convexity requires the strong first-order lower bound only when x and y project onto the same minimizer.

xπ=yπ ⇒ f(y)≥f(x)+⟨∇f(x),y−x⟩+(μ/2)‖y−x‖².

Source: Khademi (2026), Definition 6.19, p. 4243 ↗

Definition

6.20 · Weak Strong Convexity

Weak strong convexity requires a strong first-order inequality only in the direction of the nearest minimizer.

f*≥f(x)+⟨∇f(x),xπ−x⟩+(μ/2)‖xπ−x‖².

Source: Khademi (2026), Definition 6.20, p. 4243 ↗
