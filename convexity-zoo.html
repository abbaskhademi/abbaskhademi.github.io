Taxonomy at a glance

Not all classes can be placed along one linear hierarchy. Many inclusions require smoothness, a convex domain, a nonempty minimizer set, or additional geometric conditions. The guide preserves references to the original numbered statements.

Classical

Strongly convex ⇒ convex ⇒ quasi-convex (subject to standard assumptions).

Geometric

Convex ⇒ star-convex ≡ 1-quasar-convex; smaller γ allows weaker radial requirements.

Regularity

Strong convexity ⇒ PL; PL and quadratic growth have more nuanced relationships beyond convex settings.
